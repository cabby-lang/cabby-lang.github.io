The window

The playground runs everything that doesn't need the computer itself, windows and games too (use gui, use game: shown under the program, and played with the keys or the mouse). Sound, 3D, the network, other programs and threads say so when they're used; files are the program's own, kept until it ends; and there's no compiling (-c) or interactive session here. For those, download Cabby. It needs a recent browser (Chrome or Edge 137, Firefox 131, Safari 18.4 or later).
